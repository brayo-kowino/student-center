About Iskify360

Knowledge is the fuel. We build the engine.

One unified team covering software, engineering design, cyber security, and documentation.

Why we exist

Founded in Nairobi, Iskify360 was built on a simple idea: switching between vendors wastes time and money.

Whether you need an app built, a 3D model designed, or a network audited, you work with people who understand your project from day one.

No handoffs, no gaps.

One team owns your project from start to finish. You talk directly to the engineers doing the work, not through a thick layer of account managers.

We build it ourselves.

We work directly on your project. No outsourcing to third parties or hidden contractors. The people you talk to are the people writing the code and drawing the models.

Empowering your team.

We explain what we build, so your team truly understands the system, not just the end result. We believe in knowledge transfer as a core deliverable.
